## Objective
User: "splash ekranında ikon ve isim ve slogan yok... bu bütün diller için geçerli." The splash shows the icon, NShoptor and the slogan in the device language.

## Steps
1. Generate launch_image.png and splash_branding.png for each locale in lib/core/l10n/app_*.arb, using that locale's slogan, into drawable-<qualifier>-nodpi/.
2. Map ARB codes to Android qualifiers (zh-CN -> zh-rCN, zh-TW -> zh-rTW; check aapt for he and iw).
3. Build flutter build apk --profile; install; check the splash in en and tr on the emulator.
4. Keep English as the default drawable.
