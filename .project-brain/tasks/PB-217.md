## Objective
Fix real Turkish supermarket receipt failures ("Fiş okunamadı") proven with the user's A101 and ŞOK receipts across both AI Vision (`server/src/vision_tasks.js`) and offline ML Kit / deterministic parser (`ai_receipt_reader.dart`, `receipt_queue_service.dart`, `receipt_parser.dart`, `receipt_document.dart`, `ai_image.dart`), and improve the photo capture/queue UX (`receipt_capture_flow.dart`) with a sleek animated loading overlay during image compression/save and a rich localized confirmation SnackBar (all 71 ARB locales) after tapping `Tamam`.

## Steps
1. In `lib/features/receipts/ai_receipt_reader.dart`, when `parseReceiptImages` receives `AiReceiptNeedsMore` or `AiReceiptRetake`, run `scanReceiptOfflineFallback` before failing so readable receipts (e.g., cropped top header or AI math hiccup) are recovered whenever offline OCR extracts valid items/document.
2. In `lib/features/receipts/ai_image.dart`, preserve upright JPEGs properly when `jpegExifOrientation` is `0` or `<= 1`, and avoid degrading OCR-readable details.
3. In `lib/features/receipts/parser/receipt_parser.dart`, fix Turkish receipt parsing for:
   - Quantity-only rows with trailing unit pricing such as `2 x20,00 TL/ad` before `EKMEK %01 *40,00`
   - Weighed quantity rows such as `1,033Kg X 99,00` before `TADPİ PİLİÇ BAGET KG %01 *102,27`
   - Glued VAT suffixes like `ERPiL%01`, `*`-prefixed totals, `ARA TOPLAM` vs `ODENECEK TUTAR`, and bottom KDV breakdown tables (`%1 *129,00 *1,28`) + payment metadata (`KREDİ KARTI *563,50`, `YAPI KREDİ`, `Puan:0,00`) so they are not parsed as phantom product lines
   - Campaign discount trailing minus lines (`25TLFINISH110TL *149,00-`, `25TLARKO50TL *54,50-`) and structural footer links (`https://fatura.a101.com.tr`)
4. In `lib/features/receipts/receipt_document.dart`, support `ARA TOPLAM` and slightly damaged `ODENECEK T ITAR` totals, and exclude post-total KDV breakdown/card metadata rows from `productLines`.
5. In `server/src/vision_tasks.js`, clarify prompt/validation for Turkish receipts with campaign discounts (so `itemSum - discountSum === payableTotal` handles inline `*149,00-` discounts cleanly) and handle cropped headers when items, date, and payable total are clear (returning verifiable status/partial or complete appropriately without spurious rejection on legible ŞOK/A101 photos).
6. In `lib/features/receipts/receipt_queue_service.dart`, ensure headless WorkManager background retries cleanly handle plugin-channel unavailability without permanently marking recoverable drafts `unreadable`.
7. In `lib/features/receipts/receipt_capture_flow.dart` and `lib/core/l10n/app_*.arb` (all 71 locales), show an animated loading dialog/overlay with scanner/receipt icon during photo capture/compression/save, and display a styled localized SnackBar with icon and quick `Fişlerim` action after tapping `Tamam`.
8. Add regression tests for both exact A101 and ŞOK receipts + loading/SnackBar widget behavior in `test/features/receipts/**` and `server/test/**`.
