## Objective
Obtain the documents Apple requires to make NShoptor available with AI features in mainland China.

## Steps
1. Confirm with a qualified filing service or local CAC office which entity can file for the paid NShoptor iOS app. The user confirmed there is currently no mainland entity or local publishing partner.
2. Verify the exact model and provider endpoint used for each China-facing AI feature and fallback in server/wrangler.jsonc. Match it against the CAC filing registry and obtain a usage agreement for the actual provider and model route.
3. Determine any required app-level CAC registration and applicable MIIT app/ICP filing. Obtain the required proof and prepare the security assessment report from the service and data-flow record in docs/store/privacy-policy.md, reconciled against deployed behavior.
4. Submit the approved filing information, usage agreement, and assessment proof/report in App Store Connect App Compliance. Re-enable mainland China after Apple verifies the documents.

## Acceptance
- [ ] [integration] Confirmed authorized applicant plus actual provider authorization and applicable filed model/app references.
- [ ] [integration] App Store Connect accepts the AIGC declaration and the security assessment proof and report.
- [ ] [interaction] The mainland China storefront is enabled only after compliance acceptance and verified availability.

## Verify
Check the CAC registry and filing service case record, the provider authorization, the completed security assessment proof and report, and the App Store Connect availability readback.

## Escalate if
An eligible applicant, compliant provider/model route, required license, or authorization agreement is missing.

Apple's mainland China-specific instructions allow a negative AIGC declaration when the covered service is genuinely disabled or geoblocked in mainland China. The current Cloudflare Worker configuration sends AI requests to an intermediary endpoint and can fall back across Gemma, DeepSeek and Qwen. Alibaba Cloud's public Qwen filing alone does not establish that this deployment or every fallback is covered, or that Crazy Penguin has authorization.
