## Objective
Keep the existing seven-day promotional deadline on ordinary reinstall, including when app-local settings disappear. Preserve paid purchases and offline shopping.

## Steps
1. Expose an app-scoped device identifier to foreground and receipt engines.
2. Bind existing and replacement install IDs to the original Cloudflare trial start while retaining old-client support.
3. Require confirmation for fresh installs, preserve legacy deadlines and retry on resume.
4. Document trial data and verify migration, expiry and reinstall behavior.
