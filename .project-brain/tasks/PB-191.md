## Objective
Device feedback F11-F16, F19, F20 (user, 2026-10-28) — exact wording and rules in `.project-brain/references/device-feedback-2026-10-28.md`.

## Steps
1. First photo without readable header (store + date) saves nothing and asks to photograph from the top.
2. Header read → create the list at once and attach the receipt (state incomplete until the total is read); import read items.
3. Remove the global unfinished-receipt banner/dialog from home and lists; drafts without a list are not offered.
4. Add a "Receipts" section in list detail (store, date/time, total, state colour); tap incomplete → continue; "Scan receipt" offers continue-incomplete or new.
5. Allow several receipts per list; delete a receipt (and its purchases) so it can be imported again.
6. Cancelled/failed captures delete their draft and owned photos.
7. Investigate F20 (earlier receipt missing, sample items appearing) and fix the root cause with a regression test.
