2. locales API33+ installedOnDeviceLanguages/checkRecognitionSupport; API31–32 cihaz dili ve gerçek recognition error fallback. İzin sonucu async; destroy bekleyen resultları kapatır; log yok.
3. SttSpeechService tek adapter/channel callback yaşam döngüsü. SpeechService status stream, FakeSpeechService status; controller exception/disposal guard. Locale exact/aynı dil; unsupported hata; transcript korunur; ikinci tap stop.

5. Native channel mock ilk/ikinci init callbacks, yeni controller status/error, unsupported locale, exception/disposal; Android debug build. Fiziksel recognition PB-069'dadır.
