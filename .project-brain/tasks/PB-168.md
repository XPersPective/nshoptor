## Objective
Correct cross-script corruption in Telugu no-ads, not-bought, item, import-label, and other audited copy; preserve meanings and behavior, keep generated strings in sync, and retain the confirmed private annual subscription draft.

## Steps
1. In `lib/core/l10n/app_te.arb`, change `proBenefitsLine` and `planNoAds` to `ప్రకటనలు లేవు`, and `compareNotBought` to `ఇంకా కొనలేదు`.
2. Replace the exact malformed item root `ఆइటెమ్` with inflected native Telugu `అంశం` forms only in `receiptLineActions`, `linkToItem`, `itemsEmptyTitle`, `itemsEmptyBody`, `addItemTooltip`, `plannedTotalSummary`, `deleteItemConfirm`, and `clearPurchaseConfirm`; preserve `{total}`, `{count}`, and surrounding text.
3. Compare `storeLabel`, `permissionsBody`, `templateHint`, and `planFreeAds` with English and Turkish; replace foreign-script contamination with accurate Telugu while preserving the source meaning and any placeholders.
4. Compare `separateImportLabel` with English and Turkish; replace the Thai-script copy with accurate Telugu (`వేరే ప్రతిగా దిగుమతి చేయండి`) while preserving the meaning “Import as a separate copy.”
5. Regenerate `lib/core/l10n/generated/` and run the focused localization suite after all Telugu corrections.
