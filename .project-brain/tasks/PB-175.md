## Objective
"Bu faturaları doğru okuyor değil mi?" (user): a receipt may already print net product prices and also an informational discount summary. Current document and review always deduct that summary again, unlike the low-level parser. Make all layers agree without silently changing uncertain totals.

## Steps
1. Reproduce complete TRY/IDR net-price + separate discount summary through ReceiptDocument and review: total must stay the printed net total, with no second deduction. Also cover gross prices plus real deduction.
2. Add one shared pure integer discount-resolution helper/getter in receipt_parse_result.dart: preserve observed discount; apply zero only when the product sum exactly equals the known printed total, otherwise preserve the existing positive discount magnitude. Missing/ambiguous totals retain existing review/mismatch behavior; do not infer from approximate sums.
3. Use that same resolution for parser computed reconciliation, ReceiptDocument.toParseResult and review's optional discount purchase row. Preserve the observed discount metadata independently.
4. Verify actual approval/persisted purchase sum and duplicate behavior for full identified receipts; no writes before explicit approval, no new tax markers or automatic metadata fabrication.

## Acceptance
- [ ] [machine] Net-priced receipt plus discount summary persists exactly its printed total with no duplicate discount; gross-priced receipt persists one negative discount and its printed net total.
- [ ] [machine] TRY and IDR fixed-point amounts, zero, missing/unknown totals, ambiguous mismatch and existing receipt regressions pass.
- [ ] [security] Only explicit approved complete/reconciled documents write purchases; duplicate approval and malformed/partial receipt boundaries remain enforced.
- [ ] [architecture] One shared pure resolution keeps parser/document/review consistent; observed discount stays available and no DB schema/migration or other currency grammar changes.
