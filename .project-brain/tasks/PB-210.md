## Objective
Eliminate the pitch-black dark surface default (`#07140E` / `#040D09`) and overly harsh saturated action buttons (`Yeni Liste`, `Fişten Listeye`, and red/orange/blue buttons). Introduce multiple eye-friendly dark & light palette variations with calm, harmonious foreground/background button pairings while preserving brand green identity (`C-007`), pure-black opt-in (`darkBlack`), and WCAG AA contrast (`>= 4.5:1` text, `>= 3:1` UI boundaries).

## Steps
1. Update `lib/core/theme/app_theme.dart`:
   - Lift `AppTheme.brandDarkSurface` from `#07140E` to a comfortable elevated forest-slate (`Color(0xFF121C17)`, keeping `green > red` and `green > blue` for F36).
   - Provide custom non-pitch-black dark & clean light surface ladders across all `AppPalette` values (`forest`, `ocean`, `sunset`, `lavender`, plus new calm variations `sage` and `slate`) so users have distinct dark/light theme variations without harsh black voids; keep `black: true` opt-in strictly pure black.
   - Calibrate `filledButtonTheme`, `FilledButton.tonal` / `ColorScheme` secondary/primary containers across all palettes so primary and secondary CTAs look calm and harmonious without neon glare while passing WCAG AA contrast.
2. Update `lib/features/lists/lists_screen.dart` bottom CTA bar (`lists_new_list_button`, `lists_receipt_button`) and swatch labels in `lib/features/settings/settings_screen.dart` to expose the new palette variations cleanly.
3. Update `test/core/theme/theme_test.dart`, `test/core/theme/palette_test.dart`, and `test/features/settings/settings_layout_theme_test.dart` to assert elevated dark surfaces, calm action luminances, and WCAG AA contrast across every palette and light/dark/AMOLED combination.

## Acceptance
- [x] [visual] Dark theme default uses an elevated forest-slate surface (`brandDarkSurface`, not `#07140E`/`#040D09`), and pure black remains selectable via AMOLED opt-in.
- [x] [visual] At least 6 curated light+dark palette variations exist (`forest`, `sage`, `ocean`, `slate`, `sunset`, `lavender`) with visually calm surface/container contrast.
- [x] [accessibility] `FilledButton` (`Yeni Liste`) and tonal action (`Fişten Listeye`) maintain >= 4.5:1 foreground contrast and >= 3:1 surface boundary contrast on all palettes without overly saturated glare.
- [x] [interaction] Selecting any theme/palette or AMOLED switch in Settings updates the app live, persists across reload, and keeps >= 48dp button touch targets while `flutter test test/core/theme/ test/features/settings/settings_layout_theme_test.dart` passes cleanly.
