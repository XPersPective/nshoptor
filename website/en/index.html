From home to checkout,
three simple steps.

Shopping is the main task. Price comparison, voice input and reports are there when you need them.

01

Prepare your list

Name your list and add products with their quantities. Enter a home price estimate if you like; it is optional.

02

Record the market price

Tap an item and enter its market price. An item with a market price is marked purchased. A home price is optional, and 0 is a valid price.

03

Review your shopping

Check recorded amounts and price differences in the shopping summary. Explore available reports and exports in the app; some depend on your plan.

A few
useful answers.

Can I use it offline?

Basic lists and recorded prices stay on your device. AI help, purchases and store links need internet. Speech recognition and text reading depend on your device’s supported languages and installed models. Manual entry remains available.

Do I need to enter a home price?

No. You can enter a market price directly. Any recorded market price marks the item purchased, including 0. Clearing the field removes the automatic purchased state. A home price alone does not mark an item purchased.

Can I rely on receipt and shelf-label reading?

Text recognition and AI suggestions can make mistakes. Check products, quantities and especially unit versus total amounts before saving. Lighting, blur and print layouts can affect the result.

What is sent to AI?

AI help is optional and can be switched off in Settings. When used, selected task text and language are sent through our server to the provider. The server also processes quota and purchase-verification metadata. See the privacy policy for the full data flow and retention details.

Are reports and backups free?

Shopping summaries, PDF and CSV reports are core features. JSON backup and restore are included in Pro; existing Max access is also preserved. Check the current features in the app and the Google Play price before purchasing.

How do paid plans work?

The only new paid offer is an annual Pro subscription: USD 1 per year base price before applicable local taxes. Pro includes 100 AI requests each month, no ads, and JSON backup and restore. Free includes 10 requests each month. Google Play shows the final localized price and applicable taxes before purchase; the payable amount is not guaranteed to be exactly USD 1 in every country. It renews automatically each year until cancelled. Manage or cancel in Google Play. Existing Pro/Max rights and purchase restoration remain supported.
