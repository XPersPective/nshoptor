## Objective
Kullanıcı: "harcamaları geçmiş olan alttaki taba koyabilirsin… takvimi en yukarıda… açılır… önceki 3 ayın harcamaları ve haftalık harcamalar gibi içerisine birkaç tane sola doğru kaydırılarak tab koyabilirsin… ilkinde bu ay… kaydırdığın zaman diğer ayları da görebilir." · "alınan ürünler olabilir… bu ay alınan ürünler, bu hafta alınan ürünlerin listeleri… tıkladığımız zaman elmaya… grafiği, fiyat çizelgesi… evdeki tahminle gerçek arası ve kişinin o ürüne ne kadar para ödediği."

## Steps
1. `insights_repository.dart`: add optional `DateTime? from, DateTime? to` (UTC, half-open) to `purchaseStats` and `completedSpend` filtering on `sl.completed_at`; add `productPurchases(String normalizedName, String currencyCode)` returning per completed list: completedAt, planned unit price (`pi.planned_unit_price`), actual unit price (line total / quantity of the entry, or `actual_unit_price`), quantity, unit, paid minor (`actual_line_total_minor_units`).
2. New `lib/features/history/history_screen.dart` `HistoryScreen` (`HistoryPlaceholder` delegates to it); it watches completed lists so the IndexedStack tab refreshes. Top: horizontally scrollable `TabBar(isScrollable: true)` periods: `historyThisWeek`, this month (`formatMonthYear`), then the previous 11 months. Default selected: this month.
3. Under the tabs: an `ExpansionTile(key: Key('history_calendar'))` collapsed by default containing the existing month calendar (move `_MonthCalendar` to a public `MonthCalendar` in `spending_screen.dart`) for month periods; summary card: total spent for the period (`completedSpend` sum) and number of shopping trips (monthly limit stays in SpendingScreen).
4. Section `historyProductsSection`: `purchaseStats(from,to)` tiles: name, quantity+unit, paid total; tap → new `ProductHistoryScreen` (normalized name).
5. Section `listsTabCompleted`: completed lists of the period (existing tiles).
6. `lib/features/history/product_history_screen.dart`: two-line chart Evdeki vs Çarşıdaki unit price, total paid, one card per purchase (date · paid, quantity, both unit prices) and a link to the existing normalized `PriceHistoryScreen` when a product id exists.
7. Keep the insights icon to `SpendingScreen` for weekly/monthly/category charts.
8. New ARB keys `historyThisWeek`, `historyProductsSection` in all 71 ARB files with real translations via `tool/i18n/add_keys_local.py` (static dict, no network); regenerate with `flutter gen-l10n`.
9. Tests: period filter in `purchaseStats`; history tab shows this-month tab selected and product tile from a seeded completed list; tapping product opens price history with planned vs actual row.
